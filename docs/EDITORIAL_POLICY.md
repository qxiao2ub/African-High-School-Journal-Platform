# Editorial Policy

**Founder / Author:** Kavya Kaushal Shah  
**Advisor:** Dr. Qingyang Xiao


## Mission

The journal promotes high school research literacy, peer review, and publication opportunities, especially for African students and schools.

## Editorial workflow

1. Submission is received.
2. Local AI similarity screening is shown to the student/editor.
3. The platform suggests reviewers based on expertise similarity.
4. Reviewers submit recommendations and comments.
5. Editors make a final decision.
6. Accepted manuscripts are published electronically.

## Editorial independence

AI tools may recommend reviewers and flag similarity, but editors and teachers make final decisions.

## Corrections and withdrawals

The journal should maintain a process for correcting published manuscripts and withdrawing papers when serious issues are discovered.

## Data protection

Before real student use, the journal should publish a privacy policy, define retention periods, limit collection of minor data, and obtain consent where needed.

## Accessibility

The platform should aim to support low-bandwidth access, readable formatting, mobile devices, and accessibility best practices.
