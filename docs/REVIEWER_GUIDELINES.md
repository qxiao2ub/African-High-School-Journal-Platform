# Reviewer Guidelines

**Founder / Author:** Kavya Kaushal Shah  
**Advisor:** Dr. Qingyang Xiao


## Reviewer role

Reviewers help students improve research quality, clarity, ethics, and presentation. The goal is educational peer review, not gatekeeping.

## Recommendations

- Accept: suitable for publication with only editorial polish.
- Minor revision: publishable after small corrections.
- Major revision: promising but needs substantial revision.
- Reject: not suitable in current form, or outside scope, or contains major integrity/safety issues.

## Review criteria

1. Originality and student authorship
2. Clarity of research question
3. Appropriateness of methods
4. Quality of data or evidence
5. Interpretation of results
6. Ethical and safety considerations
7. Writing quality and structure
8. Correct use of citations
9. Constructive feedback for improvement

## Conflict of interest

Reviewers should decline assignments when they have a personal, financial, supervisory, or institutional conflict that could affect fairness.

## Student-centered tone

Comments should be kind, specific, educational, and actionable. Avoid harsh language.
